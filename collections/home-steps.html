How the demo works

  1. 1

    The scan

    Each page is an image in Omeka, held as an archive would hold it, with its source and its rights.

  2. 2

    Tetrak reads it

    A script sends each page through Tetrak's OCR and writes the transcript back to the page, with the release and the engine that produced it.

  3. 3

    The comparison

    Where a person has already checked a page, Tetrak's reading is scored against theirs, by character and by word, and the two sit side by side.
